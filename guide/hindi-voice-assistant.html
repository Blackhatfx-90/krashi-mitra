Vridhi AI: AI Voice Assistant in Hindi for Farmers

Vridhi AI (also written Vridhi AI) · Hindi voice support

A large number of farmers in India can farm expertly and still find a screen full of small text hard going. Vridhi AI is built so that reading is optional. Every diagnosis, every piece of treatment advice and every weather warning can be read aloud, in Hindi and in other Indian languages.

Which languages

Vridhi AI works in Hindi and English, and in Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Urdu, Nepali, Konkani, Maithili, Dogri, Bodo and Sanskrit. The language you choose changes the whole app, not just a few labels — crop names, advice, and the voice.

It speaks without internet

This matters more than it sounds. Many voice features quietly stop working the moment the signal drops, because the speech is generated on a server. Vridhi AI deliberately prefers the voice built into your phone over any network voice, so advice is still read aloud in a field with no bars. If your phone has no Hindi voice installed, the app says so rather than going silent and leaving you wondering.

What it reads out

  • The disease name and how serious it is
  • What to do about it — the organic option first, then the chemical one
  • Weather warnings, such as not spraying before rain or in strong wind
  • Frost warnings at night, when a single cold night can take a whole potato crop
  • Reminders from the crop calendar, counted from your sowing date

Long advice is not cut short

Phone speech engines stop partway through long passages — a real problem when the last sentence is the dosage. Vridhi AI breaks advice into pieces at commas and spaces rather than mid-word, keeps track of where it is, and checks every second that the voice is still speaking. If it stops early, the app picks up where it left off.

Asking by voice

You can also speak to Vridhi AI instead of typing, and ask about weather, spraying, fertilizer or mandi prices in your own words.
