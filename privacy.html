Privacy Policy

Krashi Mitra stores account details and farmer profile information needed to provide sign-in and personalized agricultural features. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, not plain text.

Crop photos are processed by the selected offline model or, when online detection is enabled, sent to the configured server-side AI provider. API credentials are never exposed to the browser.

We do not sell personal information. Use the account contact route to request correction or deletion of stored profile data.
